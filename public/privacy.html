Privacy Policy

Ludo Crown respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Ludo Crown on the web, Progressive Web App (PWA), Android, or iOS, how it is used, and how you can control or permanently delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In Account Data (Optional): You can play Offline Practice and Pass & Play as a guest without signing in. If you sign in with Google to unlock online matchmaking, friends, tournaments, and cloud sync, Firebase Authentication provides your unique user ID (UID), display name, and verified email status. Your email address is never displayed publicly or shared with other players.
  • Public Gaming Profile & Presence: Your chosen player display name, avatar, equipped board/dice cosmetics, match statistics (games played, wins, captures, ELO rating, league), and real-time lobby/in-match/offline presence are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so friends and opponents can play with you.
  • Multiplayer Rooms, Chat & Invites: Room states, in-match quick chat/emote messages, friend connections, and room invitations (which automatically expire after 2 minutes) are stored in Firestore to deliver real-time gameplay.
  • Push Notification Tokens (Opt-In Only): If you explicitly enable notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token and your alert preferences are stored privately for your account. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings or in the app.
  • Local Device Storage: Guest progress, audio mute state, arena backdrop theme, and ad privacy preferences are saved locally in browser/app storage on your device.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Ludo Crown uses trusted third-party services to operate online multiplayer and keep the game free to play:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging & Analytics):{' '} Powers secure sign-in, cloud saves, real-time multiplayer tables, opt-in push alerts, and aggregated gameplay/app-health analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Apps): Displays a top adaptive banner, optional rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch for virtual coins, gems, spins, or tickets), and occasional post-match interstitial breaks. AdMob may process device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
Privacy-First Default: Non-Personalized Ads are enabled by default in Ludo Crown. You can switch between Non-Personalized and Personalized ads at any time in Profile → Legal & Privacy → Ad Privacy Preferences.

3. Data Security & Retention

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Private wallet balances, push tokens, and private room messages are protected by strict owner-scoped Firestore Security Rules. We retain your account data only while your account remains active.

4. Children's Privacy

Ludo Crown is a general-audience casual board game and is not knowingly directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can update your display name, change your ad personalization preference, disable notifications, or permanently delete your entire Ludo Crown account and cloud data directly inside the app at any time:

  • Open Ludo Crown → Profile (top-left avatar) → Legal, Privacy & Fair Play → Delete Account & Data.
  • Deleting your account permanently removes your Firestore profile, virtual wallet, push subscription, friend connections, pending invites, and Firebase Authentication account.

Disclaimer & Fair Play Notice

  • Virtual Currency Only — No Real-Money Gambling: All Coins, Gems, Entry Stakes, Tournament Prize Pools, Lucky Wheel rewards, and Cosmetics in Ludo Crown are 100% virtual in-game items with zero real-world monetary value. They cannot be cashed out, redeemed, exchanged, or traded for real money, goods, or real-world prizes. Ludo Crown does not offer real-money gambling or wagering of any kind.
  • Entertainment Purpose: Ludo Crown is designed purely for casual tabletop entertainment and friendly competition.
  • Dice Fairness & Smart Pity Timer: Dice rolls use standard 1-in-6 odds. To keep matches fun and prevent frustrating deadlocks, a transparent Smart Pity Timer gently assists players who experience multiple consecutive turns with zero legal moves.
  • Auto-Play & Disconnect Policy: If a player disconnects or leaves a live online match, Auto-Play temporarily covers their turns so opponents are not stranded, and the player may rejoin at any time. To ensure fair play, matches completed under Auto-Play or exited early do not grant coins, trophies, or rating rewards.
  • AI Bot Players: Practice mode and unfilled online matchmaking seats may include automated AI bots, which are always clearly labeled on the board.
  • Independent Application: Ludo is a traditional public-domain board game derived from classic Pachisi. Ludo Crown is an independent game application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any other board game publisher.
